### Fictional accounting assumptions

For the synthetic revenue-addback workflow, assume:

- The identified reversal relates to a prior-year revenue accrual in the selected scope.
- A collection's supplied allocated amount belongs to that prior-year accrual; it is
  not automatically the whole receipt. Allocation is authored fixture evidence.
- The outstanding schedule is separately supplied, not generated by the evaluator
  to make the reconciliation tie.
- Positive addbacks increase reported revenue. An amount already included in the
  report is subtracted when calculating an **additional** adjustment. This is a
  reporting adjustment, not a balanced journal or proof of a ledger posting.
- Support must match the reporting cutoff, and history must be explicitly complete,
  for a within-scope pass. Stale support and unknown history remain review-required
  even in a synthetic case; simulation does not override those controls.

`examples/accrual-synthetic-scenarios.json` freezes fictional inputs and expected
treatments. These are transparent, builder-authored regression fixtures—not blind
trials, independent accountant judgments, or real-client results. Run them with
`PYTHONPATH=src python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_accrual.py -v`.

**Out of scope:** autonomous posting, live Workiva writes, broad ACFR certification,
paid model dependence, new gateway infrastructure, multi-tenant SaaS, cosmetic
redesign, and fabricated human review/time-savings claims. Client-derived cases stay
outside Git. Publication, deployments, and external sends require separate authority.
